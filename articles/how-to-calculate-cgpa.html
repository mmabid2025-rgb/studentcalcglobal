How to Calculate CGPA

CGPA is a cumulative measure of academic performance. The exact formula depends on your institution, but a common weighted approach uses grade points and course credits.

Weighted formula

CGPA = total of (grade point × course credit) ÷ total course credits.

Example

If three courses have grade points of 4.0, 3.0 and 3.7 and each carries 3 credits, the weighted average is (4×3 + 3×3 + 3.7×3) ÷ 9 = 3.57.

Do not assume every CGPA scale is 4.0

Some institutions use 5, 10 or other scales. Enter the scale stated by your institution rather than guessing.

For international admissions

A simple linear conversion can be misleading. If your target university publishes a conversion table or asks for a credential evaluation, use that method instead.
