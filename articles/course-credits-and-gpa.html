How Course Credits Affect GPA

In a weighted GPA, credits determine how strongly each course contributes to the final average.

Formula

Weighted GPA = Σ(grade points × credits) ÷ Σcredits.

Example

A 4.0 in a 4-credit course contributes more to the weighted average than a 4.0 in a 1-credit course because it carries four times the credit weight.

Always check your institution

Some programs calculate academic averages differently, so use the official academic regulations when the result matters for progression or admission.
