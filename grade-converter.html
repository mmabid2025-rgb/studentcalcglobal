German grade estimate calculator

Estimate a German grade from your university's grading scale with the modified Bavarian formula.

Enter your university grading scale

Use your home university’s official grading scale. This calculator supports only source scales where a higher number means a better result. The German result uses the usual 1.0 (best) to 4.0 (minimum passing) range for this estimate.

Estimated German grade—

Enter your scale and calculate.

Modified Bavarian formula (higher-is-better source scales only): German estimate = 1 + 3 × (highest possible grade − achieved grade) ÷ (highest possible grade − lowest passing grade)

This is an indicative estimate, not an official admission result. If your source scale uses lower numbers for better results (for example, 1.0 is the best grade), select that direction; this calculator will not convert it. Do not reverse a scale by guesswork. Universities may use different minimum-pass definitions, rounding and country- or institution-specific rules. Confirm the method with the receiving university.

How to use it

  1. Enter the highest possible grade in your home university's scale.
  2. Enter the lowest grade that allows you to pass the overall qualification.
  3. Enter your final overall grade.
  4. Compare the estimate with the target university's official conversion policy.

The modified Bavarian formula is a non-binding orientation for converting a higher-is-better source grade to the usual German grade range. The University of Bayreuth and Technical University of Munich both explain that their conversion guidance is not binding for every admission decision.

Sources: University of Bayreuth · Technical University of Munich · Pakistan HEC semester system guidelines
