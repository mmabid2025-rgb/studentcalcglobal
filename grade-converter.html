German grade estimate calculator

Estimate a German grade from your university's grading scale with the modified Bavarian formula.

Enter your university grading scale

Use the scale shown on your transcript or in your university's official regulations. For a typical descending German result, 1.0 is best and 4.0 is the minimum passing grade.

Estimated German grade—

Enter your scale and calculate.

Modified Bavarian formula: German grade = 1 + 3 × (maximum grade − achieved grade) ÷ (maximum grade − minimum passing grade)

This is an indicative calculation, not an official admission result. Universities may use different rules, country-specific references, rounding and minimum-pass definitions. Do not assume every Pakistani or Romanian university uses the same grading scale. Confirm the maximum and minimum passing grades from your own transcript or regulations and check the target university's admissions rules.

How to use it

  1. Enter the highest possible grade in your home university's scale.
  2. Enter the lowest grade that allows you to pass the overall qualification.
  3. Enter your final overall grade.
  4. Compare the estimate with the target university's official conversion policy.

For the German scale, the modified Bavarian formula is commonly used as a non-binding first orientation. The University of Bayreuth and Technical University of Munich both explain that the result is not binding.

Sources: University of Bayreuth · Technical University of Munich · Pakistan HEC semester system guidelines
