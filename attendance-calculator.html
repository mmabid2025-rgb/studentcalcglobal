Attendance Calculator

Enter whole class counts. The future-class estimate assumes you attend every next class and do not miss any more.

STUDENTCALC GLOBAL

Current attendance formula

Attendance = (classes attended ÷ classes held) × 100.

Estimate future classes

The calculator estimates how many consecutive classes you must attend to reach your target, assuming no further absences. For example, 60 attended out of 80 classes is 75%. To reach 80%, you would need to attend 20 more classes consecutively: 80 out of 100.

Check your institution’s policy

Some universities calculate attendance by course, credit hours, practical sessions or excused absences. This estimate uses the counts you enter and does not replace your institution’s official attendance record.
