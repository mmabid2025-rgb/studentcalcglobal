How weighted GPA is calculated

Each course’s grade-point value is multiplied by its credit hours. Add those products and divide by the total credit hours included.

GPA = Σ (Grade Points × Course Credits) ÷ Σ Course Credits

Why the institution’s scale matters

There is no single grading conversion that applies to every university in a country. Even universities in the same country may use different grade-point maximums, plus/minus values, pass thresholds, and course-exclusion rules. Use the grading key on your transcript.

Do not convert marks by simple ratio

Dividing a percentage or mark by its maximum and multiplying by 4.0 (or another GPA scale) is not a universally valid conversion. Use the official equivalency table published by your university or the receiving institution.
