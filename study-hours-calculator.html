About this calculator

Divide your available study time equally across the number of subjects you enter.

Formula: available study hours ÷ number of subjects. This is an equal split, not a personalized workload estimate. Adjust it for subject difficulty, deadlines and breaks.

Important

Academic rules, grading scales, attendance policies and admission formulas vary by institution and country. This tool is a general calculator, not an official university decision.
