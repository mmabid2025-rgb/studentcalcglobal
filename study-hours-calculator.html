Study Hours Calculator

Estimate how much time you could allocate to each subject when you split one study period equally.

Enter the total hours available in the same planning period and the number of subjects to include.

How to use the study hours calculator

  1. Enter the total time you can study during the period you are planning.
  2. Enter how many subjects you want to include.
  3. Calculate the equal share of time for each subject.

Formula

Hours per subject = available study hours ÷ number of subjects.

Worked example

If you have 12 study hours available and want to cover 4 subjects, the equal split is 12 ÷ 4 = 3 hours per subject. The result is a planning estimate, not a timetable.

Make the plan realistic

An equal split is a neutral starting point. It may not suit every week: a difficult subject, an upcoming exam or an unfinished assignment may need more time. Adjust the allocation yourself, include breaks, and leave room for review and practice questions.

This calculator divides time equally. It does not assess subject difficulty, learning speed, course requirements or the amount of study time your institution recommends.
