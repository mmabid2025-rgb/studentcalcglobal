Popular student questions

How is GPA calculated?

GPA is normally a credit-weighted average of grade points. Because grading scales differ, enter the grade-point values used by your institution.

Can I use these calculators outside the US?

Yes. Several tools use custom inputs, and the international tools help you review common grading conventions.

Is an online GPA conversion official?

No universal conversion is accepted everywhere. For admissions and credential evaluation, follow the destination institution or evaluator's official rules.

Do you store my marks?

The calculator tools are designed to perform calculations in your browser and do not require an account.
