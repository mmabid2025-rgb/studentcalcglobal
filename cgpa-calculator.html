How is CGPA calculated?

When a university calculates cumulative GPA using semester GPAs and credit hours, the common weighted formula is:

CGPA = Σ (Semester GPA × Semester Credit Hours) ÷ Σ Semester Credit Hours

Example: Semester 1 GPA 3.20 across 15 credits and Semester 2 GPA 3.60 across 18 credits gives (3.20 × 15 + 3.60 × 18) ÷ 33 = 3.42.

Use the right grading scale

There is no single worldwide GPA scale. Some universities use 4.0, 5.0, 7.0, 10.0, or other systems; some countries primarily use marks or reverse grading. Always use the maximum GPA and grade-point values on your own transcript or official university regulations.

Do not convert marks to GPA by simply dividing by the maximum mark and multiplying by 4.0 (or another scale). That shortcut is not a valid universal equivalency.

Important limitations

  • Semester GPAs must be on the same grade-point scale.
  • Credit hours must be comparable and included under your institution’s CGPA policy.
  • Repeated courses, failed courses, transfer credits, pass/fail courses, and withdrawals may be handled differently by each university.
  • For official admission or scholarship decisions, use your institution’s transcript or official evaluation.
