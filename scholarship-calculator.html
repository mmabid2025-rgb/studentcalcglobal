About this calculator

Build a custom weighted score from up to three criteria. Enter each score from 0 to 100, pair it with a positive weight, and make all weights total 100%.

Formula: Σ (criterion score × criterion weight) ÷ 100. Leave both fields blank for any criterion you do not use. This is a planning calculation, not an official scholarship eligibility decision.

Important

Academic rules, grading scales, attendance policies and admission formulas vary by institution and country. This tool is a general calculator, not an official university decision.
