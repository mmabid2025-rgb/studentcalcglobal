Scholarship Score Calculator

Enter scores from 0 to 100 and non-negative weights. The calculator normalises by the total weight you enter.

STUDENTCALC GLOBAL

How the score is calculated

Custom score = Σ (criterion score × criterion weight) ÷ Σ criterion weights.

Worked example

An academic score of 90 with weight 60 and a test score of 80 with weight 40 produce (90 × 60 + 80 × 40) ÷ 100 = 86.

Use the result carefully

This is a model you define, not an official scholarship eligibility test. Real scholarship programmes may use minimum grades, subject prerequisites, income criteria, interviews, document checks or their own published weighting rules. Follow the current scholarship provider criteria.
