How to Choose the Right GPA Scale

Before converting grades, identify the exact scale used by your institution. A country name alone is not enough because universities can use different grade boundaries and credit rules.

Check your transcript

Look for the maximum grade, grade-point scale, letter-grade table and credit system.

Check the target university

If the university publishes a conversion table or asks for a credential evaluation, use its method instead of a generic online formula.

Use online calculators carefully

Online calculators are useful for planning and comparison. They should not be presented as official admissions evaluations unless the method is verified.
