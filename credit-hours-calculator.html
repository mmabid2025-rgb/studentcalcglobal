Credit Hours Calculator

Enter your total registered credit hours. Add the number of teaching weeks if you also want a simple weekly average.

Use the credit total and semester length shown in your own course or academic information.

What does a credit hour mean?

Credit systems describe the academic value or workload assigned to a course. The meaning of a credit can differ between countries, institutions and programmes, so use the definition in your university's academic regulations.

How this calculator works

Enter the total credits for the study period. If you also enter the number of weeks, the calculator divides total credits by weeks.

Arithmetic weekly average = total credit hours ÷ semester weeks.

Worked example

A student registered for 15 credits over a 15-week semester has an arithmetic average of 15 ÷ 15 = 1 credit per week. This is only a division of the total; it is not a measure of classroom contact hours, independent study hours or total workload.

Before using the result

Some systems use credits to represent expected learning workload, while others define them through course or contact-hour rules. Do not compare credit totals across institutions without checking the relevant system and programme regulations.
