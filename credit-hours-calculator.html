About this calculator

Review the total credit hours in your semester and, if you enter the semester length, calculate a simple average per week.

Formula: total semester credits ÷ semester weeks. This is an arithmetic average, not a measure of weekly classroom contact hours or total study workload.

Important

Academic rules, grading scales, attendance policies and admission formulas vary by institution and country. This tool is a general calculator, not an official university decision.

Privacy

Your inputs are processed in your browser for these tools. No account is required.
