Age Calculator

How age is calculated

The calculator compares your date of birth with the current date on your device and reports completed years, months and days. A future date or an invalid calendar date is rejected.

Privacy

Your date of birth is processed locally in your browser. It is not submitted to an account or used to create a profile.

Privacy

Your inputs are processed in your browser for these tools. No account is required.
