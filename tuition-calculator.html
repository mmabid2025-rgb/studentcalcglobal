About this calculator

Estimate total tuition from a per-semester fee, number of semesters and additional costs. Enter all amounts in the same currency.

Formula: tuition per semester × number of semesters + other costs. This is a simple estimate; it does not include currency conversion, future fee increases or costs you leave out.

Important

Academic rules, grading scales, attendance policies and admission formulas vary by institution and country. This tool is a general calculator, not an official university decision.
