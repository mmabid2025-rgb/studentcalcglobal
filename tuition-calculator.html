Tuition Calculator

Enter all amounts in the same currency. Add one-time costs if you want them included; this tool does not convert currencies or estimate living costs.

STUDENTCALC GLOBAL

Formula

Total estimate = (tuition per semester × number of semesters) + other costs.

Worked example

If tuition is 2,500 per semester for 8 semesters and other costs are 1,000, the estimate is 21,000 in the same currency.

Costs to check separately

Living expenses, annual fee increases, exchange rates, insurance, visa charges and scholarships are not inferred. Add costs manually if you want them included. Confirm current fees with the university before making a budget.
