GPA vs CGPA: What Is the Difference?

GPA usually describes performance for a particular term or set of courses, while CGPA generally describes cumulative performance across multiple terms or the whole program.

GPA

A term GPA can be calculated from course grade points and credits for that period.

CGPA

CGPA combines academic performance across the periods included by the institution. Some universities calculate it directly from all courses; others combine semester GPAs.

Why scales matter

GPA and CGPA are not globally standardized. Always check the scale, grade boundaries and credit rules used by your institution.
