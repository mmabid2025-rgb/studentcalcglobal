ABOUT

Tools for students, wherever they study.

StudentCalc Global is a free collection of practical calculators for school, college and university students. The site is designed around a simple principle: make common academic calculations understandable and fast.

International by design

Education systems use different grading scales, admission formulas and attendance rules. Where possible, our calculators use custom inputs so students can enter the rules that apply to them.

Accuracy

Calculators provide mathematical results based on the values entered. They do not replace an official transcript, university policy, admissions decision or financial quote. Always verify institution-specific rules.
