Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

StudentCalc Global provides educational calculators and study information. This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit the website and how you can make privacy choices.

Information you enter into calculators

Our basic calculators are designed to process the values you enter in your browser and display a result. The website does not require you to create an account to use these tools. Do not enter sensitive personal information, passwords, or private academic records into a calculator.

Cookies and similar technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies if optional services are introduced. These technologies can help services operate, remember preferences, measure usage, or deliver advertising. Your browser settings may allow you to control or delete cookies, although disabling some technologies can affect how services work.

Advertising and Google AdSense

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled on this website, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use information about your visits to this website and other websites to provide advertisements.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this website and/or other websites on the Internet. You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also learn about and opt out of some third-party vendors' personalized advertising cookies at AboutAds.info.

When advertising is active, the advertising vendors and technologies actually used may depend on the settings and services enabled in the publisher's advertising account. We will update this policy if those services materially change.

Analytics

If analytics tools are enabled, they may collect technical and usage information such as pages visited, approximate location derived from network information, device and browser details, and interactions with the website. Any analytics service in use will be described here. We do not claim that analytics are active unless they have been enabled.

Third-party websites

Some pages may link to external websites. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies, and StudentCalc Global is not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children

This website is intended for a general student audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

Your privacy choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings and use the advertising preference links above. Where applicable law requires consent for advertising or analytics technologies, the relevant consent choices will be presented before those technologies are used.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when website features, advertising services, or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was most recently revised.
